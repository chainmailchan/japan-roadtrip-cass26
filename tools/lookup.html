Place lookup helper

For you (the curator), not your friends. It finds places on Google Maps and fills in their coordinates and Google place ID (the place ID gives the pop-up a photo and an exact “View in Google Maps” link). Two ways to use it:

  1. Fill in your sheet: in your Places tab, select everything (click any cell, press Ctrl/⌘+A), copy, and paste it below. Rows that have no coordinates are looked up; everything else is left exactly as it was.
  2. Add new places: type place names, one per line, or paste a Google Takeout list export.
